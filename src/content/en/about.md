# About JDK Factory

## Cement, made for Kuwait

JDK Factory is a **Kuwait-based cement manufacturer** supplying dependable, consistent-quality cement to contractors, ready-mix producers, precast and block manufacturers, and developers across the country.

We build our reputation one delivery at a time: the cement in every bag or bulk load should perform on site exactly as it did in our laboratory.

## What we do

- **Manufacture** Portland and sulphate-resisting cement from carefully controlled raw materials
- **Test** production batches in our laboratory before they leave the plant
- **Supply** in 50 kg bags and in bulk, arranged around your project schedule
- **Advise** on which cement suits your application, from foundations to finishes

## Who we serve

- Building and infrastructure contractors
- Ready-mix concrete plants
- Precast, block and paving manufacturers
- Developers, consultants and project owners
- Building-material traders and suppliers

## Why JDK Factory

- **Consistent quality** — controlled processes and batch testing, so performance is predictable
- **The right cement for Kuwait's conditions** — including sulphate-resisting cement for aggressive soils and groundwater
- **Reliable supply** — bagged and bulk, planned around your programme
- **Straightforward ordering** — request an order online and receive a firm quote from our team
