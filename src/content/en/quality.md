# Quality You Can Build On

## Our approach

Cement quality is decided long before a bag is filled. At JDK Factory, quality is managed at every stage — from raw materials to dispatch — so that what reaches your site is consistent, batch after batch.

## Quality control at every stage

- **Raw materials** — inspected and blended to a controlled composition
- **Production** — process parameters monitored along the manufacturing line
- **Laboratory testing** — samples from production batches tested before release
- **Packaging** — sealed bags and clean bulk loading to protect the product
- **Traceability** — batch records so a delivery can be traced back to its production run

## What we test

- **Fineness** — particle size influences strength development and workability
- **Setting time** — initial and final set, for predictable handling on site
- **Soundness** — confirms the cement will not expand after setting
- **Compressive strength** — measured on standard mortar specimens at specified ages
- **Chemical composition** — including sulphate and chloride limits

## Handling and storage on site

- Store bags off the ground, on pallets, in a dry and covered area
- Keep bags clear of walls and moisture
- Use older stock first
- Do not use cement that has hardened into lumps
